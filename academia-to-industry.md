# From academia to the real world

<p class="lede">Research fellow to quant consultant: what carried over, what didn't, and what I had to learn fast.</p>

*This page is a work in progress — I'll add to it as the transition settles.*

## What carried over

- The instinct to find the one calculation that changes the picture, and to do it properly.
- Comfort with being confused for a long time before anything becomes clear.
- Writing things down so that someone else can check them.

## What didn't

- The timescale. A PhD problem can take a year; a client question needs an answer by Thursday, and a good-enough answer on Thursday beats a complete one in March.
- The audience. Nobody on a project cares that the saddle is complex; they care what the number means and how much to trust it.

## What I had to learn fast

- Cloud infrastructure, data pipelines and the tooling around them — none of which appears in a maths PhD.
- How a decision actually gets made inside an organisation, and where a quantitative argument fits into that.
